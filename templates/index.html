How to Play Pesten

🎯 Objective

Be the first player to get rid of all your cards!

🃏 Card Basics

  • Each player starts with 7 cards
  • Play cards that match the suit or number of the top card
  • Draw a card if you can't play

🎴 Special Cards

  • 7: Play again
  • 8: Skip next player
  • Ace: Reverse direction
  • 2: Next player draws 2 cards
  • Jack: Change suit
  • Joker: Next player draws 5 cards + change suit

⚡ Penalty Rules

  • Playing a special card as your last card forces you to draw 2 more cards
  • Penalty cards (2, Joker) stack - play the same number to pass the penalty
